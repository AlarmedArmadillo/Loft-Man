Phase 0

Stop the leaks

0–30 days · quick wins
01

Claim & optimise the Google Business Profile

Categories, services, service-area towns, real job photos, opening hours, weekly posts. No map listing showed up in search at all — for a local trade, the map pack is where "loft boarding near me" actually converts, and it's free.

Impact Effort · Low Local
02

End the two-website civil war

Pick one canonical domain — .com already holds the search authority — and 301-redirect the other into it. Confirm backlinks in Search Console first, then port the sharper .co.uk title ("Loft Ladders & Hatches Wiltshire") onto the survivor.

Impact Effort · Med Technical
03

Reconcile the name, address & phone

Site says SN10 5HF; directories say SN10 5FQ. Pick the true one and make it identical everywhere — Google trusts businesses whose details match across the web, and his don't yet.

Impact Effort · Low Local
04

Switch reviews back on

A follow-up text + a QR "leave-a-review" card handed over at the end of each job, pointing at the GBP. 53 reviews at 4.92★ is a genuine asset sat idle — make it compound instead of frozen.

Impact Effort · Low Trust
05

Sticky click-to-call & WhatsApp on mobile

A thumb-reachable call button and WhatsApp on every page. Most trade enquiries are phone calls and most of this traffic is mobile — right now the only route is a contact form, which quietly loses the impatient buyer.

Impact Effort · Low Web / CRO
06

Put the proof above the fold

Which? Trusted Trader badge, the star rating, "13+ years · fully insured · guaranteed" — visible before any scroll. He's earned the trust signals; the site buries them.

Impact Effort · Low Web / CRO
07

Stand up measurement first

GA4, Search Console, and call tracking before we change much else. Baseline the decline so every later move can be proven — and so we learn whether leads are calls, forms, or WhatsApp.

Impact Effort · Low Measure
Phase 1

Lay the foundations

1–3 months · make him findable
08

On-page SEO pass

A real title, meta description and single H1 on every page, written around how people search ("loft boarding Devizes", "loft ladder fitted Wiltshire"). Currently generic and, across two domains, duplicated.

Impact Effort · Med Technical
09

Local landing pages per town

One genuine page each for Devizes, Marlborough, Chippenham, Trowbridge, Melksham, Calne — targeting "loft boarding [town]". This is how a small trade outranks nationals in its own patch.

Impact Effort · Med Local
10

Structured-data markup

LocalBusiness, Service, AggregateRating and FAQ schema. Gets the star rating and key facts into the search result itself — free real estate and a click-through boost.

Impact Effort · Low Technical
11

Citations & directory cleanup

Consistent listings on Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yell, Checkatrade, Bark, FreeIndex — reusing the reconciled NAP. Reinforces the local signal and mops up the conflicting-address mess.

Impact Effort · Med Local
12

Technical health check

Page speed, mobile rendering, Core Web Vitals, HTTPS, sitemap, indexation. Dated builds are usually slow and clunky on a phone — which both Google and buyers punish.

Impact Effort · Med Technical
13

Before / after job gallery

Real photos of finished boarding, ladders and hatches, grouped by service. Trade buyers buy with their eyes — a cluttered loft turned into clean storage sells better than any paragraph.

Impact Effort · Med Web / CRO
Phase 2

Modernise & convert

3–6 months · turn visits into calls
14

Rebuild the website, mobile-first

Fast, modern, one clear value proposition — the current site looks its age and that alone costs trust before a word is read. Everything above lands harder on a site that looks like it belongs in 2026.

Impact Effort · High Web / CRO
15

Shorten the enquiry path

A three-field quote request — postcode, job type, phone — with instant confirmation, replacing the long contact form. Every extra field is a lost lead; make saying "give me a price" effortless.

Impact Effort · Med Web / CRO
16

"From £X" pricing guidance

Indicative ranges per service. Qualifies buyers, filters tyre-kickers, and answers the one question every visitor has — without committing him to a fixed quote.

Impact Effort · Low Web / CRO
17

Advice content that captures research

Plain-English answers: "how much does loft boarding cost?", "will boarding my loft cause condensation?", "boarding vs a full conversion". Catches people before they're ready to buy — and feeds the FAQ schema.

Impact Effort · Med Content
18

Rebuild the Google Ads account

Call-only and local-intent campaigns, a tight radius around Devizes, call/location/sitelink extensions, a proper negative-keyword list, and landing pages that match the ad. Same budget, far more of it spent on people who'll actually book.

Impact Effort · Med Paid
19

Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed)

Pay-per-lead placement above the map, with the green trust badge. Built for exactly this kind of local trade — he only pays for genuine enquiries, and the badge does trust-work for free.

Impact Effort · Med Paid
Phase 3

Compound & de-risk

6–12 months+ · a business, not a phone
20

Systematise the referrals he already lives on

An ask-script, referral cards, and a small thank-you for the introduction. He relies on word-of-mouth but leaves it to chance — turn the strongest existing channel into something repeatable.

Impact Effort · Low Retention
21

Reactivate past customers

Email/SMS the customer list for insulation top-ups, a "winter loft check", or extra storage before Christmas. Your Mango Media wheelhouse — the cheapest lead is a happy customer he already has.

Impact Effort · Low Email
22

Seasonal campaigns

Insulation before winter, storage before Christmas, decluttering in spring. Loft demand has an obvious calendar — get ahead of it instead of waiting for the phone.

Impact Effort · Med Email / Paid
23

Referral partnerships

Estate agents, insulation grant schemes, and solar / heat-pump installers who need loft access done properly first. Other people's customers, arriving pre-trusted.

Impact Effort · Med Brand
24

Become the known "loft man" of west Wiltshire

Consistent brand and a steady trickle of short job videos on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. Owning the name locally makes every other channel cheaper — people search for him, not the service.

Impact Effort · High Brand
25

Systemise for succession

A documented process, a tracked lead pipeline, and delegated (not shared-password) account access. He's in his 60s — a business that doesn't live entirely in his head is one that can take a holiday, hire help, or one day be sold.

Impact Effort · Med Brand

Before any of this — set access up properly

Don't take his passwords. Add yourself as a delegated user / manager under your own login on Google Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics and Google Ads (link his Ads account to a Manager / MCC). On IONOS, keep him as owner and change the password afterward if there's no delegated option.

It protects him, it covers you, and nothing breaks if either of you ever needs to revoke — the professional move when someone hands over the keys to their livelihood.

Where the leverage really is

Phases 0–1 are cheap, fast, and where most of the lost ground gets recovered — a claimed map profile, one website instead of two, live reviews. Do those before spending another pound on ads. The redesign and paid rebuild in Phase 2 then convert the traffic those foundations earn, and Phase 3 turns one-off jobs into a system that keeps producing without him chasing it.
