⚑ What you need from Simon — get it in week 1

  • Delegated access to Google & IONOS (his logins stay his).
  • The one true address & postcode — settles the NAP conflict.
  • Job photos (before/after) for the gallery and GBP.
  • His past-customer list and where the 53 reviews currently live.

✓ What Simon should expect on day 30

  • Delivered: one site, live optimised map profile, reviews growing, tracking, town pages.
  • Early leads: the GBP, reviews and click-to-call can produce enquiries within the month.
  • Still compounding: organic rankings & lead volume build over 60–90 days — set this expectation now so month one isn't judged on SEO alone.

The one risk to manage out loud

Client delay is the only thing that breaks this plan — access, the address confirmation, or photos arriving late. Ask for all four in the Week 1 kickoff, in writing, and the GBP clock and the build both start on time. Everything else is within your control.
