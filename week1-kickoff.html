Getting unblocked
so the 30-day clock starts clean

You can't start a single deliverable until Simon's access and materials are in hand — so this is the real first job. Get everything below sorted in the first few days and the whole sprint runs on time. The one thing that can't wait is the Google verification.

⚑ The moment you have access

Fire the Google Business Profile verification (step 1) first. The postcard/code can take 5–14 days — start it on day one and it clears mid-month; leave it and it's the thing you're stuck waiting on in week 4. Everything else can be worked while that clock ticks.

01

Book one 20-minute setup call

The shortcut for an older client

Don't email Simon a list of login instructions — for someone in his 60s that's friction and a support headache. Do the access together on one short screen-share (or in person), you talking him through each click. It's faster, it's reassuring for him, and you walk away with everything set up in one go. The tables below are your checklist for that call, not a to-do list to send him.
